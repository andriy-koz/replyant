Replyant writes about one subject from two angles: how AI agents and automation earn their keep inside a real business, and how to engineer them so they keep working after launch.

The [Blog](/blog/) is written for operators and executives. It covers AI strategy, ROI measurement, governance, compliance, pricing, and the hire-versus-automate decisions that land on a leadership team's desk.

The [Lab](/lab/) is written for engineers. It covers agent architecture, tool calling, the Model Context Protocol, retrieval, evals, observability, and the agent security research behind our work on prompt injection and tool poisoning.

Every topic below links to the posts tagged with it, from both sections. Pick the one closest to the decision or system you are working on, or start with [AI Agents](/tags/ai-agents/) for the broadest view.
